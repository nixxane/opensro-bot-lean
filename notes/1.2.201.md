# Panel bottom rows stay on screen

- On screens like 1920×1080 with browser bars, the bottom of the bot window (the feedback line and the wide window's activity log) was cut off. It now always stays on screen.
- The scrolling part of the window shrinks to fit instead, and the window re-fits when you move it, resize the page, restore it or show it again (Shift+F7).
- On short screens the wide window uses a smaller activity log and status strip to leave room.

> Contributed by duan78 (#81). Tested in simulation, and in a local browser test page at several window sizes. Not tested in a live game.
