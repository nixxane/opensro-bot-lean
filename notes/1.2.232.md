# Leveling finds spots for new characters
- Zero-setup leveling now knows many more hunting spots, including the thinly spread first-level monsters around each town (Mangnyangs near Jangan, for example). There were none for level 1 before, so a new character stayed on its saved spot.
- A character with no spot at its own level nearby now takes the nearest one a level or two up.
- With leveling spots on, a walk back to an old saved spot (a town centre, say) turns to the spot for your level at once, instead of walking to the old one first.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
