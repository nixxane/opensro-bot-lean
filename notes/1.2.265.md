# Cave fixes: party follow and Jangan Cave giants
- Follow a party member now works inside caves: the bot walks to the member in the cave instead of sending a move to the wrong place. It still does not follow through a cave entrance or a teleport.
- Giants that spawn as party monsters (as in Jangan Cave) are hunted again. Before, they counted as Party monsters, which are off by default, so the bot ignored them. They now have their own "Giant party" switch under Monster classes, on by default; old settings follow your Giants switch.
> Tested in simulation only; not yet tried in a real cave.
