# Sell Star: withdrawals and walking fixed

- Fixes Sell all Star gear stopping with "a withdrawal did not reach the bag". It now waits for the gear to show up in the bag, in whatever slot the game puts it.
- It no longer insists on reaching the exact saved standing spot: close enough to the warehouse or merchant is enough, and if it cannot find a way it tries from where it stands.
- Only tested in simulation, not on the live server.
