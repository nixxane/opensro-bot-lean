# Leveling always walks back
- With **Level up by itself** on, the character now walks back to its hunting spot after a death or a town trip. It no longer uses or buys Reverse Return Scrolls: leveling spots are only a short walk from town.
- The world statue is not needed for leveling, and the Item mall's reverse buying is paused while leveling is on.
- Turning leveling on or off during a town trip ends that trip.
- With leveling off nothing changes: **Back to the hunting spot** and the reverse settings work as before.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
