# Lure stops near players or after set pulls

- Two new lure options under Combat > Hunt area > Lure: Stop when this many other players are within 30 m, and Stop after this many completed pulls. Both are 0 (off) by default, so nothing changes unless you set them.
- The player option stops the lure when that many strangers are within 30 m of your character. You and your party members never count.
- The pull option stops the lure after the chosen number of pulls. A round where nothing was pulled does not use up a pull, and the count starts again each time you start.

> Contributed by duan78 (#85). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
