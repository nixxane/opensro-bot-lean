# Party support can restore party MP
- Party → "Heal and buff the party" adds an MP heal: a single-target MP heal skill and a group MP heal skill, each with its own "below %" threshold, so a Bard can refill party members' MP when it runs low.
- A "Heal my MP too" switch restores your own MP. Other members' MP reads from the party window in steps of 10%; your own is exact.
- MP heals run with the HP heals and buffs, one cast at a time, and never walk to members.
> Tested with the integration suite only; not exercised in a live game.
