# Auto quest says what it is waiting for

- When Start quests is on but nothing happens, the Quest page now says why: no quest markers, no NPC offering a quest, an NPC in another region, quests excluded by your lists, or what it is waiting on.
- The same reason is written to the log when it changes.

> Only tested in simulation. This explains the idle state; it does not yet walk to NPCs in other regions.
