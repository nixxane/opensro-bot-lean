# Leveling replaces Seal gear too
- With Level up by itself on, Seal (SOX) and other rare pieces were never replaced, so a Seal blade, sword or a level 1 Seal necklace stayed on for good while ordinary pieces were upgraded. They are now replaced like any other piece when the town sells a higher level. The Seal piece goes to your bag and is never sold.
- Gear above +3 and gear you protect are still never replaced.
- If the walk to a picked hunting spot fails five times in a row, the bot stops trying new spots instead of wandering the map. Reaching a spot resets the count.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
