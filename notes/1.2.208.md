# Sell all Star gear from the warehouse

- New button on the Town page: Sell all Star gear in warehouse. One click takes every Seal of Star piece out of the warehouse and sells it at your saved repair merchant, in batches that fit your bag, then stops. It never runs on its own.
- Needs the warehouse and repair merchant saved on the Town page, and you standing where both are in reach. Moon and Sun gear, buff-set gear and Keep names stay in the warehouse.
- Only tested in simulation, not on the live server.
