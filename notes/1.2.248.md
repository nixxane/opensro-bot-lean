# Leveling reaches Donwhang, and the pet collects the drops
- The move to the next town never got there: its walk from Jangan to Donwhang tried to cross the river on foot, failed, and the bot then left Donwhang alone for the session. A character past level 24 kept hunting around Jangan, sometimes at a spot with no monsters. The move now uses the world statue's teleport (Jangan to Donwhang, later Donwhang to Hotan), then makes the new town the return point as before.
- Any walk the bot makes between places (not only the way to the hunting spot) now takes a ferry or teleport when one is needed. Auto trade still walks.
- With Level up by itself on and a pickup pet summoned, the pet now collects the drops, gold and gear included, instead of the character running for them. This happens once per character, and only when pickup was still on its default. If you switch it back by hand, it stays.

> Tested in simulation with the game's real route data, not in a live game.
