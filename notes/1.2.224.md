# Reliability fixes for death, rebirth and quests
- If you die or change world while a buff-gear swap is pending, hunting can now start anyway. The gear is restored quietly once it is safe, not during town trips or open trade, stall or shop windows.
- Rebirth is now re-sent every 10 seconds while your character is still dead.
- Quest data that fails to load is retried after 10 minutes instead of on every tick.
- The reconnect message no longer includes your character's name, so it stays out of reports.
- Smaller fixes: the pet-bag-full toast works with the chime off, Max skills stops when you leave the world, and settings are saved when you close the page. The town tier is reset when you switch character.
- No new option.

> Contributed by duan78 (#96). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
