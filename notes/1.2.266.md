# Alchemy success sound with a custom MP3
- New on the Alchemy page: turn on a sound for a confirmed enhancement success (off by default). Failures, destroyed items, stone fusions and attribute-stone fusions stay silent.
- Pick an MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A under "Custom alchemy sound..." (up to 1 MB, kept in this browser); choosing a file turns the sound on. "Use built-in chime" returns to the short built-in chime and "Test alchemy sound" previews it.
- The sound also plays when the target + is reached, and a new hit restarts the clip instead of stacking it.
> Tested in simulation only, not in a live browser or game.
