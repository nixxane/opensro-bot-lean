# Seal gear is picked up first

- When several drops are on the ground, Star, Moon and Sun gear is now collected before everything else, whatever the distance. Sun goes before Moon, and Moon before Star.
- This applies to both your character and your pickup pet. Among drops of the same kind, the nearest still goes first.
- Without the item list loaded, the nearest drop still goes first. Ownership, exclusions and bag space still apply as before. There is a short note about this on the loot options in the panel.

> Contributed by duan78 (#80). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. The Sun, Moon, Star order was tested for Sun before Star only.
