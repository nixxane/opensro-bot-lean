# Move pet loot to your bag
- New option under Loot, Bag and pet space: "Move pet loot to my bag when the pet is nearly full". The pickup pet's items move to your bag one at a time, keeping two bag slots free, so the pet can keep collecting and the bot stays at the hunting spot longer.
- The town trip starts once the bag is full as well. The weapons and shield chosen under Combat never move. Off by default.

> From a player request. Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
