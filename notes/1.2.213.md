# Choose which monsters to avoid for kill-stealing

- New option "Avoided monsters" under "Avoid other players' targets" in the hunting settings. "All monsters" is the old behavior. "Normal & champion only" still leaves alone normal and champion monsters another player is fighting, but takes giant, party, elite and other strong monsters even if someone else is fighting them.
- The default is "All monsters", so nothing changes unless you pick the other choice. If "Avoid other players' targets" is off, the new option has no effect.
- The "no target" explanation in the bug report counts monsters by the same rule.

> Contributed by duan78 (#91). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
