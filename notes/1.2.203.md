# Anti-stuck can go to town

- New option under Settings > Anti-stuck: Go to town instead of stopping. When the bot makes no progress after all its recoveries, it makes a town trip instead of stopping.
- The trip ends in town and does not return to the spot where nothing worked. It needs the town cycle to be on, and it is off by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it on.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
