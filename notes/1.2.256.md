# Pick your own sound for SoX drops
- Hunting → Loot has a new "Custom SoX sound…" button under the SoX chime. Choose an audio file (mp3, wav, ogg, up to 1 MB) and the bot plays it instead of the built-in chime when it picks up Star, Moon or Sun gear.
- "Use built-in chime" puts the old sound back. Test SoX chime plays whichever sound is set.
- The sound is saved in this browser only, so it is not part of shared or exported settings.
