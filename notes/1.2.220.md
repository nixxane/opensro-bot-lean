# Pet loot now sells again on town trips
- Town trips no longer stall at the smith when your pet is carrying loot. The server refuses sales straight from the pet's bag, so the bot now moves each sellable pet item to a free slot in your own bag and sells it from there.
- If the server refuses a move, that item is skipped and the rest are still sold. If your bag has no free slot, the loot stays in the pet and the trip carries on.
- No new option; protected and valuable items are still left alone.

> Contributed by duan78 (#92). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
