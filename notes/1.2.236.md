# Leveling empties the pet in town even with a full bag
- With Level up by itself on, a town trip now sells the bag's spare gear first. This makes room for the pet's loot, so a full bag no longer stops the trip with "free a character bag slot for storage and refills", and the pet is emptied in town.
- With leveling off nothing changes: bag selling stays a setting.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
