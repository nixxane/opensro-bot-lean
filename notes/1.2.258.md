# Auto party invite by name or nearby
- New "Auto-invite players" option in Party, off by default. It invites the players you list by name whenever they are in view.
- Optional "Also invite anyone nearby" invites everyone within the radius you set (30 m by default).
- Only invites while you are solo, the party leader, or in a party that lets members invite, and never into a full party.
- A player who does not answer is asked again after 30 s, then 1, 2, 4 minutes and so on, up to 10 minutes, so nobody gets spammed.
> Tested in simulation only.
