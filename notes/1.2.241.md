# A blocked town merchant tries another spot
- "Town cycle stopped: Blocked on the way to the repair merchant" with nothing in the way: the saved standing point beside a merchant can be a place the first trip never checked (inside a forge or a fence). The trip now tries other spots around the merchant, nearest to the way in first, before giving up. A spot that works replaces the saved one.
- If every spot fails, behaviour is as before: the warehouse is skipped, other services stop the trip.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
