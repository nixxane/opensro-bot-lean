# Leveling goes to town for gear less often
- Town gear unlocks over a few levels (in Jangan the level 16 tier opens with the weapon and boots at 16 and ends with the chest at 21). With Level up by itself on, a trip just for gear used to happen at almost every level. It now waits until the last piece of the tier is open, then buys the whole set in one trip.
- Any trip that happens anyway (after a death, for potions or repairs) still buys whatever is already open.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
