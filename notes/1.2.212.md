# Option to not pick up Star gear

- New option under the Loot settings: Equipment: don't pick up Star gear (Moon and Sun still are). When it is on, the bot and the pet leave Star (Seal of Star) equipment on the ground and keep picking up everything else, including Moon and Sun gear.
- It is off by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it on. A Pick rule on an item still takes that item.
- Only tested in simulation, not on the live server.
