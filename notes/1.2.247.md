# Spots across the river take the ferry
- A hunting spot across the river from Jangan (level 21 and up) made the bot check a walking route north into the Water Ghost Marshland, where there is no way across. The route data for one area can hold both banks of a river, and the bot took that to mean it could walk there. It now measures the real walk on the route data from both ends. When a spot cannot be reached on foot, or only the long way round, it takes the ferry: from Jangan, the China East Ferry to the far bank.
- This is decided before the slow route check, so the character no longer stands waiting for a walk that would never work.
- A ferry or teleport fee now counts for more when choosing the way, so the bot prefers a 500 gold ferry over a 5000 gold statue teleport. It pays that fee on every town trip.
- Auto trade keeps walking as before (no teleport or ferry takes a trade transport).

> Tested in simulation with the game's real route data, not in a live game.
