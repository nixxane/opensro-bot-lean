# Auto Quest: quests done for you
- New Quest page with "Do quests automatically" (Auto quests group). The bot follows the quest markers the game shows, walks to the quest NPC, accepts the quest, hunts the monsters that count, and turns it in.
- "Accept quests their NPC offers", "Only quests named" and "Never quests named" let you limit which quests it takes. Which dialogue answer belongs to which quest, and which monsters count, are learned while you play.
- Quests that can be closed from the reward window are rewarded without walking. If the reward is refused, the bot waits a minute before trying again, then goes to the NPC. A quest it cannot identify is never answered by guessing.
- The quest journal on the same page shows each quest's count, with Skip/Unskip and Abandon (click twice to confirm). It also tells you when the targets are not around.
- Your hunt area is not moved for you. Gathering, capture traps and travelling to a quest's monster spawn are not automated.
> Contributed by duan78 (#75). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. The NPC talk and reward commands were not checked against the live server.
