# Live dashboard on your phone
- New optional companion in the `live` folder: a page for your phone with each character's status, HP and MP, XP and XP per hour, gold, kills and the recent activity log.
- It runs on your own machine with Node.js and needs its own small userscript; setup, the phone link and troubleshooting are in `live/README.md`. Nothing in the bot itself changes in this version.
- The phone link is private: keep the link and its `.code` file to yourself, and close the hub window to stop it.
> Optional companion only. Tested on macOS with one character; not tested on Windows, Firefox or Safari.
