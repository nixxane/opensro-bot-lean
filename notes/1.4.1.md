# Team edition rounded out
- Optional unique converge: when a unique appears and its position is known, a hunting character moves its hunting spot there (toasts say so); the saved spot comes back when the unique dies or after 20 minutes. Off by default.
- The public pool grows a memory: with public sharing on, the unique tracker also reads and writes the relay's public store, so every room keeps the spawn history.
- Bug reports now include a team summary line (shared entities, combat edges, tracked uniques, known players).
- The repository README describes this fork's team edition.
> Still simulation-tested only; no live run with real characters yet.
