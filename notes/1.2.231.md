# Zero-setup leveling now handles skills and stats
- With **Level up by itself** on, the bot now also raises your weapon's mastery with your level (a sword or blade trains Bicheon, a two-handed staff Wizard, and so on), learns each of its skills as soon as the game allows, and spends stat points: Strength, or Intelligence for a caster. Masteries or a stat share you already chose are kept.
- Each newly learned attack skill joins your attack rotation and each new self buff your buffs, once. A skill you take out stays out.
- Auto mastery has a new choice under "Skills to upgrade": **Every skill of my chosen masteries**, which also learns new skills.
- A new switch in the leveling box turns this part off.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
