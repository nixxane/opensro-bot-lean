# A completely full bag goes to the warehouse instead of stopping the trip
- A character whose bag had filled up all the way (no free slot) used to arrive in town and stop there with "free a character bag slot", even when the warehouse could take things out of its bag. With "Sell items from the character bag" off, that happened on almost every late trip. The warehouse now goes first and makes the room, and the trip carries on.
- A bag with nothing the warehouse takes still stops the trip with the same message, as before.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
