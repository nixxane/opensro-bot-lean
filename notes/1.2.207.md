# Party joining can wait for Start

- New option under Settings > Party: Only join and accept parties while the bot is running. When it is on, Auto-recruit, Auto-accept party-matching joins and joining the party leader's party all wait until you press Start. This fixes the bot joining a party after it was stopped.
- It is off by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it on. Accept resurrection is not affected.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
