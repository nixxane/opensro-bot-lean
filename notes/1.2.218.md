# Item names show even without game data files
- The Items page now shows real item names instead of `#numbers` when the site does not serve its item data files. The bot learns each item's name and icon from what the game sends: your bag, worn gear, pet bag, warehouse, shop offers and drops on the ground.
- Item groups and filters on the Items page (potions, pets, gear and so on) now work from those learned names too.
- What the bot learns is saved in your browser, so names are still there after a reload. Nothing is added to your settings and there is no new option.

> Contributed by duan78 (#90). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
