# Zero-setup leveling
- New on the Setup page: **Level up by itself**. The bot picks a hunting spot for your level from the game's monster spawn data, near your town, and moves to a better one as you level.
- On town trips it buys the next tier of NPC weapon, armour and (optionally) accessories, wears them in the shop and sells the old pieces back. Seal gear, rare items, +4 and higher, and anything you protect are never replaced. A gold reserve (5,000 by default) is never spent.
- When better gear becomes affordable, the bot goes to town for it by itself, once.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. Off by default.
