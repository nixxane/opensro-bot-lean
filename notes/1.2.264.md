# Live dashboard inside the bot
- New in Settings: Live dashboard. The bot sends a small snapshot of itself (level, HP/MP/XP, state, position, session totals, recent activity) to your own hub every 3 to 60 seconds, default 10, so a phone can watch it.
- Your hub can be a Cloudflare Worker on your own Cloudflare account (free plan is enough) or the live/hub.js program behind an HTTPS tunnel. Nothing goes through nixxane bot servers.
- Setting up the Worker needs no install: press "Set up free on Cloudflare" in Settings, Live dashboard (or the Deploy to Cloudflare button in live/README.md), open your new hub, press "Create my private link", then "Pair NIX in this browser". Scan the QR code on that page to open the dashboard on your phone.
- Pairing: the hub gives you a pair link. Opening it in the game browser asks you to confirm in the NIX window; nothing is saved or sent until you press Pair.
- Off by default. A hub that refuses the code switches it off instead of retrying forever, and a rate limit is waited out.
- The hub address and code are kept in this browser only; they are not part of shared settings, backups, bug reports or the log.
- The separate NIX live reporter script still works for a hub on the same PC.
> Only tested in simulation: the bot's sending and pairing were tested against a stub and the local hub, and the Cloudflare Worker (setup page, QR code, pairing) only in a local Miniflare run. The Deploy to Cloudflare button has not been used, the Worker has not been deployed to a real Cloudflare account, and nothing was tried from a phone or from the real game page.
