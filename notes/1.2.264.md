# Live dashboard inside the bot
- New in Settings: Live dashboard. The bot sends a small snapshot of itself (level, HP/MP/XP, state, position, session totals, recent activity) to your own hub every 3 to 60 seconds, default 10, so a phone can watch it.
- Your hub can be a Cloudflare Worker on your own Cloudflare account (free plan is enough) or the live/hub.js program behind an HTTPS tunnel. Nothing goes through nixxane bot servers.
- Setting up the Worker needs no install: press "Set up free on Cloudflare" in Settings, Live dashboard (or the Deploy to Cloudflare button in live/README.md), open your new hub, press "Create my private link", then "Pair NIX in this browser" (on the computer where you play). Scan the QR code on that page to open the dashboard on your phone.
- Pairing: the hub gives you a pair link. Opening it in the game browser asks you to confirm in the NIX window; nothing is saved or sent until you press Pair.
- Off by default. A hub that refuses the code switches it off instead of retrying forever, and a rate limit is waited out.
- The hub address and code are kept in this browser only; they are not part of shared settings, backups, bug reports or the log.
- The live feed shows what the bot did (kills, loot, potions, deaths, town trips, reconnects) and leaves out asset warnings and the window's own messages. HP and MP never read above 100 %, and the report now also carries HP/MP values, session totals, the current target and the hunting spot. Totals read 0 before the first hunt instead of a dash.
- New dashboard look, made for phones: Cards or List view (switch at the top), a summary of bots online and total XP and gold per hour, and clear Hunting, In town, Walking back, Dead, Stopped and Offline states.
- The Cloudflare button needs a free Cloudflare account and a free GitHub (or GitLab) account.
- The separate NIX live reporter script still works for a hub on the same PC.
> Tested with one character on a real Cloudflare deploy (Deploy button, pairing, reports). The new dashboard design, the HP/MP fix and the feed filter were tested in simulation and on a local hub, not yet on the real game.
