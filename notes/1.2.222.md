# Full bags now sell before pet loot on town trips
- With "Sell ordinary gear from the character bag too" on, the bot now sells your bag items at the repair merchant first, then moves the pet's loot into the freed slots and sells it.
- This fixes trips where a full bag left pet loot with no free slot, so nothing was sold.
- With that option off, nothing changes. Items the merchant refuses are still skipped, and the rest are sold.

> Contributed by corevider (#94). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
