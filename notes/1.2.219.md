# Town trips no longer stop at the smith after a skipped supply
- Fixes the character standing still at the repair merchant at the end of a town trip with "Final supply, repair or travel-scroll check failed".
- It happened when a supply was skipped on purpose in the shop (a merchant that does not sell Reverse Return Scrolls, HGP potions or pet kits, or gold that ran short). The last check still counted that supply as missing. Skipped supplies no longer block the walk back or the reverse.
