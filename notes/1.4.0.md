# Team edition complete (phases 2-6)
- Live shared world: each character publishes the interesting monsters it sees (champion and up, party mobs, uniques with an HP estimate), the other players in view, and who attacks what (observed from every cast around); the map draws it all as a dimmed team layer, own view always winning.
- Unique tracker: the game's own server-wide announcements plus shared sightings build a history per unique with respawn windows (the timer restarts at death; 3-6 h per unique), toasts, a sound, map pins, and a Hunt here button that moves your hunting spot to the last known position.
- Player knowledge: every player the team sees is remembered with position and level; a player attacking a teammate outside a party is marked hostile (red on the map, flagged in the panel).
- Team drops: the room shares pickup counters.
- Team decisions: assist (fight what a teammate or party member fights), spot claims (a warning when two characters start hunting on top of each other), synchronized town trips (optional; a teammate's town trip starts yours), and resurrection claims (the dead member waits up to 60 s for the peer walking over instead of reviving itself).
- The room key holders always see everything; an optional public pool (per-relay, per-category checkboxes) shares monsters, players and unique events with every room on your relay — never your own position or inventory.
- The reference relay grows a room key-value store (the team's shared memory, persisted across restarts) and a console page (/?key=room key) showing the fleet with Stop everyone and Town trip buttons.
> All of it is tested in simulation and against a local relay only; a live check with real characters is still to do.
