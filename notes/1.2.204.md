# Town trips sell surplus supplies

- New option in the town trip settings: Sell surplus restocked potions above the refill amounts. It is off by default.
- When on, the bot sells picked-up or overbought stacks of the supplies it restocks (HP, MP and vigor potions, pills, reverse scrolls, HGP potions, pet kits) at the repair merchant, down to the refill amount.
- Only the extra above the refill amount is sold, largest stack first. Anything the merchant refuses is kept and the trip carries on. Nothing else in your bag is touched by this option.

> Contributed by duan78 (#82). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
