# Leveling picks better spots, raises the elements and uses an imbue
- A leveling character no longer stays on a low-level spot beside the town. The bot now looks up to four times the "Spots at most this far from town" distance (at most 5 km) before settling for a spot far below your level. At level 5 near Jangan it now takes the level 5 spot, not the level 2 one.
- Zero-setup leveling also raises the Cold, Lightning and Fire masteries after the weapon's mastery (skipped when the character has none), and their new skills join the rotation. A list you chose yourself is left alone.
- A learned weapon imbue skill is put in the empty imbue slot once. Taking it out by hand keeps it out.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
