# Leveling starts walking back sooner
- After a town trip, "Walking to the hunting spot: loading route data" could keep a leveling character standing still for up to two and a half minutes while the route was checked against the game map. With Level up by itself on, it now waits at most 20 seconds, then walks in legs as it does when no route data is available.
- With leveling off nothing changes: the full route check wait stays.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
