# Optional hunt-area search sweep
- New "Search the hunting area" switch under Combat > Hunting area. With no monster in view, the bot walks the area in a north–centre–east–centre–west–centre–south sweep so spawns beyond the view distance are found instead of waiting at the centre.
- Off by default; the existing "Return when idle" behaviour stays. Only active while hunting a saved spot with no target, and combat, loot and party support always take over first.
- Works in dungeons like Jangan Cave, where the hunt area can be larger than the view distance.
> Tested in simulation only, not in a live browser or game.
