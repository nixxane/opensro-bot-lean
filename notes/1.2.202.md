# Bug reports say why the bot stopped

- A bug report now lists the last reasons the bot stopped by itself (for example a town trip that timed out), how long ago, and whether you stopped it last.
- Before, a stopped bot only said "Bot stopped.", so reports of the bot doing nothing after a town trip could not say what ended the run.
- This only adds information to reports. Nothing about how the bot hunts or travels changes.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
