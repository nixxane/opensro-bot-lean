# Declines party resurrection when town is needed
- On the Party page, "Accept resurrection from party members" now checks whether a town trip is needed before saying Yes.
- If the Town page's "Revive in town and go back to the hunting spot" is on and you are out of return scrolls or gold, your gear is broken or worn out, or a chosen potion has run out, the bot says No to the party resurrection and revives in town instead, so the town services can refill you.
- A new Party option, "Revive in town instead when town is needed", is on by default; turn it off to keep accepting party resurrections as before.
- It works while hunting and while stopped, and stopped hunting stays stopped.
- This is on by default for anyone using Accept resurrection with the town cycle; while stopped it revives you in town even when On death is set to Wait or Stop.
> Contributed by NiftyOps (#110). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the No answer to the resurrection question has not been checked on a live server.
