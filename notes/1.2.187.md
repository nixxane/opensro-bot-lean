# Attack pet joins your fights
- New "Attack pet" group on the Combat page, under Hunt area. It is off by default. Turn on "Attack pet assists my targets".
- Every monster the bot fights is also given to your attack pet, so it no longer stays on an old monster.
- "Call the pet back before a new target" is on by default. A pet farther than the call-back radius (15 m by default, changeable) is called to your side first, then sent in. Monsters chasing it come along.
- Between targets the pet stays at your side. If you release a monster that is still alive, the pet lets it go too.
- Only the attack pet is used. The pickup pet is not touched and keeps collecting. Nothing is sent during town, restock, walk-back or resurrection trips.
> Contributed by duan78 (#73). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. The pet command was not checked against the live server.
