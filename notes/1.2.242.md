# Leveling has no distance limit for its spots
- The "Spots at most this far from town" setting is gone. With Level up by itself on, the bot picks the best spot for your level anywhere the game's route data connects to your town, and walks there however far it is. Without route data for the area it looks up to 5 km, so it never picks a spot on another continent.
- The level still counts far more than distance, so it does not cross the map for one level.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
