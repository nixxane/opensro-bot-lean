# Leveling moves on to the next town
- With Level up by itself on, a character no longer stays in its first town forever. Once that town sells nothing better and the next town's gear is open (Jangan's ends at level 21, Donwhang's next tier opens at 24), it walks to the next town's world statue, makes it the return point, and sets up its warehouse, merchants and statue there. From then on its town trips, hunting spots and gear come from the new town: Jangan, then Donwhang, then Hotan.
- If the character dies on the way, it revives as usual and tries again later. If the move fails any other way, it stays where it was and does not try that town again until the bot is reloaded.
- A new switch, "Move to the next town when this one has no better gear", is on by default.
- Not in NIX Deck's headless mode yet.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
