# Item Mall supplies and battle scrolls
- New options on the Supplies page, all off by default. The bot can buy potion bags (HP and MP) from the Item Mall when your stock runs low.
- Mall buying uses a silk budget per bot run. At 0 it never buys. Merchant town trips stay the main supply, and nothing is ordered during town trips, restocks or open shops.
- If the mall refuses an item, the bot waits a minute before trying it again. If an order is never confirmed, mall buying stops until you re-enter the world.
- The bot can keep the 20% damage and 20% resistance scrolls active, using one from your bag whenever the 30-minute buff runs out. Their packs can also be bought from the mall under the same budget.
- Reverse Return Scrolls are a new gold restock item at the merchant. If the merchant does not sell them, the bot skips them with a notice.
> Contributed by duan78 (#74). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. Only reading the mall catalogue was checked on the live server. Buying with real silk has not been tried.
