# Leveling moves to Donwhang when started in Jangan, or after a death
- With the character in Jangan (recalled by hand, or revived there after a death), starting the bot did the town services and then walked back to the old Jangan hunting spot, even when Donwhang was due (level 24 for Chinese characters). The move to Donwhang only rode on trips started in the field. Now any town trip carries it: after the services in Jangan, the bot takes the world statue teleport to Donwhang, makes Donwhang the return point, shops there and hunts there.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
