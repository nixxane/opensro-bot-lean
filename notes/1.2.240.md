# Faster route check on repeat trips, and clearer leveling gear buying
- The route check now remembers every stretch that walked, so after the first trip to a spot the way back no longer waits for it. While the character only waits for the check, it also runs faster.
- Leveling gear buying needs one free bag slot per piece instead of two, so a nearly full bag no longer leaves out rings or armour.
- Each leveling town trip now logs why a piece is not bought (kept because it is enhanced, rare or sealed; already as good as town sells; or the town has none at your level). At level 8 the town sells only a weapon, shield, gloves and rings; chest, boots and the rest come later.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
