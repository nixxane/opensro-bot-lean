# Hunting spot changes with your level
- New "Spot by level" group on the Map page. It is off by default. Turn on "Switch the hunting spot by level".
- Set a hunting spot, type the level it is for under "From level", and press "Save current spot". Saved spots are listed below with a Remove button, and are kept per character.
- The highest saved level at or below yours is your hunting spot. When you level into a new one, the bot switches to it and walks there. Below your lowest saved level, your current spot is left alone.
- It only switches while the bot is hunting. Nothing changes during town trips, restocks, travel, quest visits or resurrection. Hunt radius and filters stay as they are.
> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game. The walk to a far-away new spot was not covered by the tests.
