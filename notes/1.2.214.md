# Town trips can wear better gear you carry

- New option under Loot, in "Bag and pet space": "On town trips, wear carried gear with better stats; store the replaced piece". At the warehouse, a carried piece whose real stats beat the worn one in the same slot is worn, and the replaced piece is deposited in the warehouse, never sold.
- Stats are compared the way the game's item tooltip shows them (rolls, plus level and known blues), so a well-rolled ordinary piece can beat a poorly rolled Seal piece. Weapons are judged on the attack type of the weapon you wear now.
- Your chosen weapons and shield, buff-set gear, the alchemy item and Keep-rule items are never swapped. Pieces with an incomplete tooltip or an unmet level requirement are skipped, and a swap the game refuses is skipped for the session.
- It is off by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it on.

> Contributed by duan78 (#86). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the gear swap itself was not checked on the live server.
