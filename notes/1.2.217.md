# Alert chimes for players, death and more
- New "Alert sounds" group on the Loot page with four short chimes: another player comes near, your character dies, you level up, and a unique monster comes into view.
- Each chime has its own switch and all of them are off by default, so nothing changes unless you turn one on.
- The player chime uses the same detection as the player alert. If the browser blocks sound, one click in the game unlocks it.
- The switches are saved per character like your other settings.

> Contributed by duan78 (#88). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game; the sounds themselves were not heard in a real browser.
