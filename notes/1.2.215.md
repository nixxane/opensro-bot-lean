# No XP can move hunting to the next spot

- New option "When there is no XP" on the Town page, next to "No XP for (minutes)". "Return to town" is the old behavior. "Move to the next level spot" moves hunting to the next entry in your level spot list and restarts the no-XP timer.
- If there is no higher level spot left, the bot makes the normal town trip. Other reasons (supplies, bag, gear wear) still send the bot to town.
- The default is "Return to town", so nothing changes unless you pick the other choice. It needs "No XP for a while" and the town cycle turned on.

> Contributed by duan78 (#83). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
