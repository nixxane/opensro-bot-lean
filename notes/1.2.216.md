# Choose how long to wait for a party revive
- New option "Wait for a party resurrection (s)" in the Party settings, right under "Accept resurrection from party members". It sets how long the bot waits after dying before it revives itself in town, from 5 to 600 seconds.
- The wait only applies when "Accept resurrection from party members" is on and a living party member is in view. With nobody around, the bot still revives after 5 seconds.
- The default is 20 seconds, the same as before, so nothing changes unless you pick another value. The Activity log now shows the wait you chose.

> Contributed by duan78 (#87). Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
