# Leveling: a rescued merchant trip keeps going, and unreachable spots are skipped
- When a merchant's standing spot was blocked and the bot found another one that worked, the trip ended a moment later with "Settings or character changed". It now carries on and the working spot is kept.
- With Level up by itself on, if the walk to the chosen hunting spot gives up (a spot behind a river or cliff), the bot skips that spot and tries the next best one instead of staying put.
- Small clean-ups: a leftover distance setting is gone from the defaults, and the wide settings panel lays out its boxes faster.

> Tested in simulation only, not in a live game.
